# LeadFlow AI Employee — Installation & Operating Guide

**Product:** LeadFlow AI Receptionist & Follow-Up System  
**Series:** OCG LAB — AI PRO SERIES  
**Positioning:** Train Your Own AI Lead Employee

## What this product does
LeadFlow helps you build the operating memory and rules for an AI employee that handles lead reception, qualification, follow-up decision support, lead context, appointment rules, routing and escalation.

It is intentionally **not another CRM**. Your existing CRM, calendar, email, phone/SMS and other business tools remain the systems of record.

## The operating sequence
**BUILD → TRAIN → PRACTICE → CONNECT → DEPLOY → MANAGE → IMPROVE**

### 1. BUILD
Name your AI employee and enter your business profile.

### 2. TRAIN
Teach the employee how your business operates in normal language. Include:
- business hours and service areas
- services/products and approved pricing language
- qualification criteria and priority rules
- questions the AI should ask
- information that must be collected
- appointment and scheduling rules
- escalation and emergency rules
- team / routing rules
- brand voice and communication style
- statements the AI may or may not make
- human intervention triggers
- follow-up preferences

Corrections are training too. Examples:
- “Don’t ask that anymore.”
- “Ask this before you schedule them.”
- “We now service Derby.”
- “Never schedule inspections after 5 PM.”

### 3. PRACTICE
Run realistic scenarios before using the trained instructions with prospects. Confirm that the employee asks the right questions, respects scheduling rules, recognizes urgent situations and does not invent information.

### 4. CONNECT
Identify the business tools the employee is allowed to use. A connection should only be treated as active when it is technically connected and authorized. LeadFlow must never claim a CRM, calendar, mailbox, phone/SMS provider or social account is connected merely because you selected it in the setup interface.

### 5. DEPLOY
Use the readiness gate. When ready, export your **LeadFlow AI DNA** file from the app. Keep this file as your portable business-operating profile.

## Installing the AI DNA in a capable AI assistant
Use an AI environment that supports persistent instructions/projects/custom agents and, when you want real actions, the business connectors you authorize.

Create a persistent AI employee and give it the LeadFlow AI DNA plus this operating instruction:

> You are my LeadFlow AI Employee. Treat the attached LeadFlow AI DNA as the current operating rules for my business. Follow its business profile, instructions and permissions. Maintain lead context across interactions when the environment supports persistent memory. Do not invent lead history, appointments, pricing, service availability, policies or connector results. If a required business rule or piece of lead information is missing, say what is missing. If an action requires a connector that is not actually available and authorized, explain that clearly instead of pretending the action occurred. For every lead, be able to summarize: what the person wants, relevant history, what has already been done, missing information, appointment status, follow-up status, risks and the recommended next action. Treat later owner corrections as newer authority and update the working instructions accordingly.

### Connector rule
The AI may only perform actions that the chosen AI environment can actually execute through an authorized connector. Examples can include email, calendar, CRM, business messaging and other supported tools. If the connector is unavailable, the AI should draft the action or tell the owner exactly what still needs to be done.

## Daily operation
Use natural questions instead of hunting through screens:
- “What is going on with Robert?”
- “Who needs my attention today?”
- “Which leads are waiting on us?”
- “What information is missing before we schedule Sarah?”
- “Draft the next follow-up for David based on his history.”

## Improve continuously
When the AI gets something wrong, correct the rule rather than only correcting one answer. Update LeadFlow and export a fresh AI DNA when the operating rules materially change.

## Safety and truthfulness
LeadFlow is an operational assistant, not a substitute for professional judgment. The AI must not fabricate:
- customer or lead facts
- appointments or completed actions
- current prices or policies
- legal or regulatory conclusions
- integration status
- performance metrics

The business owner remains responsible for the final operating rules, permissions and actions.

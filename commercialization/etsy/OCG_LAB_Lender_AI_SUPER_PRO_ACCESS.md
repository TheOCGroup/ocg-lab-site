# OCG LAB
## Lender AI SUPER PRO — Buyer Access Guide

### Start here

Thank you for purchasing **Lender AI SUPER PRO**.

You do not need to install software or know how to code. Your product is browser-based.

### 1. Open the Digital Playbook

**Lender AI Digital Playbook**  
https://ocg-lab-products.vercel.app/playbooks/lender-ai-super-pro/

Start with **Setup & Connections**. The guide explains your profile, AI setup, optional email/calendar/CRM/social connections, camera/microphone permissions, and your first workflow.

### 2. Open the Lender AI SUPER PRO workspace

**Production Workspace**  
https://ocg-lab-products.vercel.app/lender-ai-super-pro/

Use this workspace when you are ready to run the content workflow.

### 3. Complete your profile

Add your name, company/brand, primary market, NMLS/disclosure language, natural voice, audiences, and preferred call to action.

### 4. Connect the AI provider you choose

The product supports multiple provider routes. Choose the provider you want to use and follow the in-product instructions.

Your AI provider may require you to create an API key in its developer/API settings. An API key is **not** your normal account password.

**Security rule:** enter provider credentials only inside the product connection screen. Never email an API key to OCG LAB, send it to support, or paste it into a chat.

Third-party AI accounts, subscriptions, API usage, and provider charges are separate from your OCG LAB purchase.

### 5. Run your first workflow

Follow this exact sequence:

1. Save your lender profile.
2. Connect your AI.
3. Run **Content Scout**.
4. Choose a recommended topic.
5. Generate the script.
6. Review the script.
7. Approve the script.
8. Choose **Record Myself**.
9. Allow camera and microphone when your browser asks.
10. Record your video.
11. Review playback.
12. Download the recording.

If a step is locked, complete the step directly above it first.

### Optional connections

The Digital Playbook also explains how common business connections work, including email, calendar, CRM, social/publishing, camera/microphone, and optional avatar tools.

A connector is only available when that actual connector appears in the live product. The guide will not pretend an unsupported integration is connected.

### Ask Harper

Harper is the Lender Digital Playbook Specialist. Use Harper to:

- explain a lesson;
- find the right prompt;
- show an example;
- practice a borrower or Realtor conversation;
- rehearse a video;
- understand what to do next.

Harper is your guide inside the playbook. The separate Lender AI SUPER PRO workspace performs the production workflow.

### Important professional-use notice

Lender AI SUPER PRO is an educational and workflow tool. It does not approve loans, determine eligibility, promise rates, replace required disclosures, replace compliance review, or replace professional judgment. Verify current mortgage facts and borrower-specific information before use.

No physical item will be shipped.

# OCG LAB
## Lender AI SUPER PRO — Buyer Access Guide

### Start here

Thank you for purchasing **Lender AI SUPER PRO**.

You do not need to install software or know how to code. Your product is browser-based.

### 1. Open the Digital Playbook

**Lender AI Digital Playbook**  
https://ocg-lab-products.vercel.app/playbooks/lender-ai-super-pro/

Start with your lender profile and Setup & Connections. Harper explains the playbook, prompts, practice workflows, optional tools and what to do next.

### 2. Open the Lender AI SUPER PRO workspace

**Production Workspace**  
https://ocg-lab-products.vercel.app/lender-ai-super-pro/

Use this workspace when you are ready to research content, create and approve scripts, use the 36-workflow Mortgage Content Studio, record in your browser, or prepare a BIGVU Production Packet.

### 3. Complete your profile

Add your name, company/brand, primary market, NMLS/disclosure language, natural voice, audiences and preferred call to action.

### 4. Connect the AI provider you choose

Choose a supported provider and follow the in-product instructions. Your provider may require an API credential from its developer/API settings. An API credential is **not** your normal account password.

**Security rule:** enter provider credentials only inside the product connection screen. Never email an API key to OCG LAB, send it to support, or paste it into a chat.

Third-party AI accounts, subscriptions, API usage and provider charges are separate from your OCG LAB purchase.

### 5. Understand BIGVU

BIGVU is optional third-party video-production software and is **not included** with Lender AI SUPER PRO.

If you already pay for BIGVU, think of the two products this way:

- **Lender AI SUPER PRO:** mortgage content strategy, Content Scout, lender-specific prompts/scripts, verification gates and human approval.
- **BIGVU:** optional teleprompter, recording, captions, editing, Brand Kit, B-roll and publishing tools, depending on your BIGVU plan.

Lender AI SUPER PRO does not claim a live BIGVU integration. It prepares the approved mortgage-specific content that can be taken into your own BIGVU workflow.

### 6. Run your first workflow

Follow this sequence:

1. Save your lender profile.
2. Connect your AI.
3. Run **Content Scout**.
4. Choose a recommended topic.
5. Generate the script.
6. Verify current mortgage facts and required disclosures.
7. Review and approve the exact spoken script.
8. Choose one production path:
   - **Record Myself** in the browser; or
   - **Copy BIGVU Production Packet** and use your own BIGVU account.
9. Record/edit the video.
10. Review the final video against the approved script.
11. Publish only after final human/company review.

If a step is locked, complete the step directly above it first.

## Etsy description

Run the numbers before you make the offer.

The **OCG LAB Real Estate Investor Calculator Bundle** gives you three dedicated browser-based deal analysis tools in one purchase:

- **Fix & Flip Calculator** - model ARV, purchase price, rehab, holding/closing costs, 70% Rule MAO, projected profit, ROI and downside scenarios.
- **Rental Property Calculator** - analyze monthly cash flow, cap rate, returns, financing assumptions and DSCR.
- **BRRRR Calculator** - model Buy + Rehab + Rent + Refinance + Repeat, including refinance proceeds, cash returned, cash left in the deal, equity, cash flow and DSCR.

### What you receive

After purchase, Etsy delivers the branded **OCG LAB Access Guide PDF**. Click the access button in the PDF to open the official 3-in-1 bundle page and choose the calculator you need.

### Why this bundle

Instead of forcing three investment strategies into one generic spreadsheet, each calculator is designed around its actual decision workflow. Change your assumptions, see the results update, and stress-test the numbers before committing capital.

### Works in your browser

- No spreadsheet formulas to maintain
- No subscription
- One-time purchase
- Desktop and mobile browser access
- Reusable for multiple deal analyses

### Important

These calculators are decision-support tools based on the assumptions you enter. Always independently verify property comps, repair scope, stabilized rents, taxes, insurance, financing, lender requirements, title, permits, closing costs and field conditions. This product is educational and operational software, not financial, legal, tax, lending or investment advice.

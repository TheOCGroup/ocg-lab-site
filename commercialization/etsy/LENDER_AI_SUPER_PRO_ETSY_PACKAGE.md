## Buyer FAQ
### What exactly do I receive?
A digital Buyer Access Guide with direct access to the live Lender AI Digital Playbook and Lender AI SUPER PRO workspace. No physical product is shipped.

### Is ChatGPT or another AI subscription included?
No. You use your own supported AI provider account and are responsible for that provider's subscription or usage charges.

### Is BIGVU included?
No. BIGVU is separate third-party software. If you already use it, Lender AI SUPER PRO can prepare approved lender scripts and production packets for your BIGVU workflow. BIGVU features, plan limits and pricing are controlled by BIGVU.

### Why use this if BIGVU can generate scripts?
BIGVU is a broad video-production platform. Lender AI SUPER PRO adds the lender-specific operating layer: mortgage audience strategy, content research, lender profile, recent-topic history, mortgage guardrails, verification requirements, Realtor/borrower workflows, approval gates and a specialized 36-workflow Mortgage Content Vault. The goal is to improve what goes into the video before production.

### Do I need coding experience?
No. The product provides plain-English setup guidance. Some AI providers require a purchaser-owned API credential from the provider's developer/API settings.

### Does it connect to email, calendar, CRM, social media or BIGVU?
A service is only considered connected when an actual connector and authorization flow exist in the live product. Guidance about a service does not mean the account is connected.

### Can I record video without BIGVU?
Yes. The production workspace includes browser self-recording after script approval, subject to the camera/microphone capabilities and permissions of the purchaser's browser/device.

### Does this replace lending compliance or professional judgment?
No. Purchasers remain responsible for licensing, employer/company rules, disclosures, advertising requirements, privacy, fair-lending obligations, current mortgage facts, borrower-specific analysis and final human review.

### Can I use it on mobile?
The Digital Playbook is responsive for modern mobile browsers. Production features such as browser recording depend on the device/browser. BIGVU usage depends on the buyer's own BIGVU plan and supported devices.

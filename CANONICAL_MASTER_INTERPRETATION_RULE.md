# OCG LAB — CANONICAL MASTER INTERPRETATION RULE

Status: MANDATORY

When the founder names an approved OCG LAB product, playbook, storefront, delivery package, design, workflow, architecture, or other artifact as the reference and says a new item should be like it, match it, follow it, use it, or be the same as it, that named reference is the literal canonical master unless the founder explicitly requests a redesign.

Default behavior:
- preserve the approved visual system
- preserve the approved information architecture
- preserve the approved interaction pattern
- preserve the approved delivery pattern
- preserve the approved storefront presentation pattern
- change only the product-specific content, specialist identity, profession or industry examples, authorized workflows, and explicitly approved details

Do not reinterpret a named approved master as loose inspiration. Do not redesign, restyle, simplify, modernize, experiment, create a new palette, create a new layout family, or substitute an inspired-by version without explicit founder approval.

For OCG LAB Digital Playbooks, the Insurance Agent AI Digital Playbook remains the canonical product master.

For OCG LAB Digital Playbook Etsy presentation, the approved Insurance Agent Etsy presentation is the canonical storefront visual master unless another approved storefront master is explicitly named.

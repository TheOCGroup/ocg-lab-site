02 · Redevelopment Vision

More than one viable story.

The property has been studied through multiple development lenses. These are feasibility concepts—not promises of cost, rent, occupancy or value—but they demonstrate how differently the same shell could be activated.

Concept Study

Residential-over-commercial

The Revit study explores a first-floor lounge/gallery configuration with second-floor apartment use and an expanded balcony / roof-access concept. This direction leverages the building’s historic proportions and downtown character while separating public and private uses by floor.

All residential or mixed-use conversion assumptions require fresh code, egress, accessibility, fire/life-safety, structural and municipal review before reliance.
Feasibility Scenario

Executive suites / coworking

A separate scenario explores private offices, premium offices, team/flex suites, dedicated desks, virtual-office memberships, conference/training space, content production and shared amenities.

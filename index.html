HOW OCG LAB WORKS

From problem to working system.

01Start with the outcomeDefine the job, decision, or bottleneck that needs to improve.
02Choose the right productUse the smallest capable tool—calculator, playbook, AI product, worker, or system.
03Operate with guardrailsConnected capabilities stay scoped to the permissions and evidence the workflow actually requires.

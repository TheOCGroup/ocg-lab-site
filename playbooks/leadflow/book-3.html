Chapter 8

Permissions &
real actions.

The owner controls what the AI can do. High-impact actions need understandable boundaries.

OFF

Not allowed

The AI can explain or draft, but cannot execute.

ASK FIRST

Approval required

Prepare the action, then wait for owner approval.

AUTHORIZED

May execute

Only through a real, supported connector and within trained rules.

STATUS

Tell the truth

PROPOSED → APPROVED → ATTEMPTED → SUCCEEDED → FAILED.

REPLIED

STALLED

Conversation went quiet

GAP

Tell the owner which missing business rule blocked the response.

Proactive Tool

Tell me what
needs attention.

Lead Attention Brief

Review the current lead data [DATA] and produce a low-noise attention brief. Prioritize unanswered new leads, replies needing action, overdue follow-up, missing information blocking progress, repeated reschedules, stalled conversations, opt-outs/suppression and training gaps. For each: why it matters, urgency, recommended next action and whether the action is only proposed or can actually be executed.

How the Specialist Works

Operational.
Not decorative.

CAN · Interpret + organize

Turn natural-language training into operating instructions, maintain coherent lead context and identify missing knowledge.

CAN · Act when connected

Use supported providers only within explicit owner permissions and report proposed, attempted, succeeded or failed states honestly.

WILL NOT · Invent

No fabricated customer facts, business policies, prices, appointment availability, connector results or completed actions.

WILL · Escalate

Unknown, urgent, sensitive or prohibited situations move to a human according to the trained rules.

LEADS

What you need to know

SCHEDULING

What is allowed

Appointment types, availability rules, restrictions and human approval boundaries.

VOICE

How you communicate

Brand tone, terminology, statements allowed and statements prohibited.

Business Training Interview

Interview me like you are onboarding a new AI lead employee for [BUSINESS]. Ask only one logical group of questions at a time. Build structured instructions for services, hours, service areas, pricing language, qualification, required lead information, scheduling, escalation, routing, brand voice, prohibited statements and human-intervention triggers.

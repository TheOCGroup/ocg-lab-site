Chapter 4

Capture &
qualify.

Fast response matters, but qualification should follow the business's real rules rather than a generic script.

CAPTURE

Identity + source

Name, contact details, channel and how the lead arrived.

NEED

What they want

Service/product, urgency, location, timing and relevant context.

QUALIFY

Business-specific criteria

Ask only questions the owner has approved and actually needs.

NEXT

Clear advancement

Schedule, collect missing information, nurture, route or escalate.

Chapter 5

Practice before
deployment.

A safe test center should reveal where the AI is ready and where the owner still needs to train it.

BASIC

Normal inquiry

Does it capture and qualify without sounding robotic?

CHANGE

Interrupted / redirected

Can it preserve context when the prospect changes subject?

UNKNOWN

Missing policy

Does it admit the business has not trained that answer yet?

HUMAN

Escalation

Does it recognize when a person must take over?

Practice Tool

Pressure-test
the employee.

Practice Test Center

Act as a demanding prospective customer for [BUSINESS TYPE]. Test my trained AI employee on: basic inquiry, missing information, price question, change of subject, appointment request, reschedule/cancel, angry response, returning lead and human escalation. After each scenario score: accuracy to training, conversational quality, missing business knowledge, permission risk and what I should train next.

Chapter 6

Follow up
with context.

Follow-up is not a blind sequence. It should stop, change or escalate when the relationship changes.

WHY NOW

Reason

Missing information, estimate sent, no response, appointment confirmation or requested callback.

HISTORY

Use context

Reference the actual conversation and avoid repetitive generic messages.

STOP

Respect replies

Stop or change automation when the lead responds, opts out or no longer fits.

ESCALATE

Know when to hand off

Human attention should be visible, prioritized and explainable.

Contextual Follow-Up Decision

Then draft the next permitted message in my brand voice. Do not claim it was sent; label the action PROPOSED unless a connected provider confirms success.

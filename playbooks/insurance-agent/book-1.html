Chapter 3

Win the first
five minutes.

Fast does not mean generic. A strong first response proves you understood why the prospect reached out and gives them one easy next step.

DO

Acknowledge context

Reference the actual request, property, family change, business need or timing issue.

AVOID

Template energy

“Just checking in” and overly polished language often signals automation instead of attention.

DO

Ask one useful question

Make the next interaction easy. Do not send an intake questionnaire disguised as a text.

DO

Set expectation

Tell them what will happen next and when they should expect it.

First Response Builder

Help me respond to a new insurance lead using the OCG LAB playbook. I will paste the lead's actual message or notes after this prompt. Use only the facts I provide. If one critical detail is missing, ask me no more than two simple questions first. Then create: (1) a concise text reply, (2) a short email, and (3) a voicemail script. Reference why the lead reached out, ask one useful question, and end with one clear next step. Never invent coverage, price, limits, deductibles or carrier rules.

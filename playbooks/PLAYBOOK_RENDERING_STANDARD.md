# OCG LAB Digital Playbook Rendering Standard

Status: APPROVED
Approved reference: Real Estate Investor AI Playbook
Canonical reference route: /playbooks/real-estate-investor/

## Permanent cover rule
Every future OCG LAB Digital Playbook must use a native HTML/CSS cover as the default production standard.

The cover must:
- occupy the page, or nearly the full page, as part of the book layout;
- render natively in HTML/CSS so text, lines and core branding remain resolution-independent;
- not depend on a raster cover image being stretched to fill the page;
- not use a floating mockup, dashboard screenshot, decorative product graphic, or detached image in place of the book cover;
- preserve the OCG LAB brand hierarchy, series label, playbook title, product subtitle, and product-specific tagline;
- remain crisp on desktop, mobile, high-DPI and Retina displays;
- use the current Real Estate Investor AI Playbook cover implementation as the visual and technical reference unless the founder explicitly approves a new standard.

## Page-fit rule
The cover belongs inside the digital book. It should visually fill the left cover page or sit very near full-page size, with no large unused white field that makes the cover appear undersized or detached.

## Quality gate
A playbook cannot be called launch-ready until the live rendered cover has been visually inspected in production. Source-code references, deployment status and asset dimensions alone are not sufficient evidence of visual approval.

Release-blocking failures include:
- blurry cover text or branding;
- raster enlargement artifacts;
- blank cover area;
- undersized/floating cover;
- cropped title, subtitle or tagline;
- cover design that does not visually belong to the page;
- use of a prior mockup/marketing graphic instead of the production book cover.

## Product parity
The Insurance Agent Digital Playbook remains the content/interaction master for structure and functionality where applicable. The approved Real Estate Investor implementation establishes the production rendering standard for the cover and first-spread presentation.

## Change control
Do not replace or materially change this cover standard across future playbooks without explicit founder approval.
